## PORT-001 — Primary hiring proposition

### Recommended positioning

**Professional identity**

> AI Solutions Architect and Product Leader

**Value proposition**

> I turn ambitious AI ideas into production systems—from discovery and architecture through delivery and adoption.

**Supporting credibility line**

> 8+ years across AI solution architecture, product management, and data science. Currently Manager, AI & Data at EY.

**Selected role emphasis**

> AI Solutions Architect · AI Product Manager · AI Forward Deployed Lead

### Why this direction

- “AI Solutions Architect” reflects current enterprise architecture and delivery work.
- “Product Leader” preserves the unusually strong product-management thread across ProjectHero, Jar, DeepConverse, and independent products.
- “Production systems” differentiates the work from strategy-only and prototype-only profiles.
- “Discovery and architecture through delivery and adoption” describes the verified end-to-end scope without implying that Ronak personally performs every engineering task.
- The existing “systems that work” language remains the memorable central promise.

### Alternative if targeting architecture roles more narrowly

**Professional identity**

> AI Solutions Architect and Delivery Leader

**Value proposition**

> I architect and lead the delivery of production GenAI systems—connecting business problems, product decisions, and reliable technical execution.

This version sends a more senior enterprise-delivery signal but makes the product-management career history less prominent.

### Alternative if targeting product roles more narrowly

**Professional identity**

> AI Product and Solutions Leader

**Value proposition**

> I take AI products from an ambiguous business problem to a working, measurable system—shaping the strategy, architecture, and delivery along the way.

This version broadens product-role fit but weakens the immediate architect signal.

### Language to retire or demote

- **“AI Systems · Product Thinking · Strategy”** — retain as supporting taxonomy if useful, but not as the primary identity.
- **“I design and deliver agentic platforms, AI products and enterprise solutions”** — accurate in spirit but too category-heavy to carry the entire supporting message.
- **“Then ask the awkward questions before production does”** — strong voice; better used lower in the hero or in “How I operate” after the professional identity is clear.

### Decision

Selected primary positioning:

> **AI Solutions Architect and Product Leader**

The portfolio will be shaped for AI Solutions Architect, AI Product Manager, and AI Forward Deployed Lead opportunities. It will demonstrate relevant fit through its work and capability framing, but will not state that Ronak is “open to opportunities.”

2. José — reliable agent architecture and hands-on systems thinking
3. Tandem — product judgement and end-to-end independent building
4. Crumbs — optional experiment demonstrating curiosity; placement still to be decided

Each project preview should state:

- What it proves
- The problem
- Ronak's role
- The outcome or honest current status

#### 5. How I operate — “How does he move from ambiguity to outcomes?”

Recommended stages:

1. Discover — identify where AI can create measurable value
2. Shape — turn the opportunity into a product, scope, and business case
3. Architect — design for reliability, security, evaluation, and reuse
4. Deliver — lead implementation, stakeholder alignment, and production handover

“Adopt” could replace or follow “Deliver” if Ronak has strong evidence of change management and sustained adoption. Do not claim that capability solely because a system was handed over.

- Core: discovery, solution design, agent architecture, product strategy, technical delivery, stakeholder ownership
- Technology: LangGraph, RAG, evaluation, Python, FastAPI, Databricks, Azure, PostgreSQL, React, Supabase

#### 7. About — “What is he like to work with?”

Keep the whiteboard-to-terminal idea, reduce credential repetition, and focus on working style and motivation.

#### 8. Contact — “What should I do next?”

Provide direct access to:

- Résumé
- Email
- LinkedIn
- GitHub

Use one consistent primary CTA across the page.
