# Portfolio recommendations

## Status

Deferred. These ideas are intentionally not part of the current homepage update.

## Goal

Keep the simpler, more credible structure while restoring some of the character and visual energy of the previous version. The portfolio should sound like Ronak speaking plainly about his work, with a small number of memorable ideas rather than a slogan in every section.

## Recommended direction

### 1. Use one memorable idea per section

Most of the copy should remain direct and conversational. Each major section can carry one stronger thought to create recall without making the whole page feel like a campaign.

Possible directions:

- Hero: AI products that survive contact with the real world.
- About: Moving from building models to owning the wider problem.
- Work: Eight years of getting closer to the whole product.
- Projects: Where I test my opinions by building.

These are starting points, not approved final copy. Any eventual wording should be tested against how Ronak naturally describes his work.

### 2. Restore visual contrast

The simplified page is clearer but more rhythmically uniform. A later design pass could add energy through layout rather than more copy.

Potential changes:

- Give the hero a stronger composition or more deliberate use of scale.
- Use a dark or coloured treatment for At a glance.
- Make company names and dates more distinctive in the Work section.
- Give each featured project a clearer visual identity.
- Treat smaller experiments as a compact, more playful group.

### 3. Use real opinions as the source of voice

Short observations drawn from experience will sound more authentic than marketing lines. These could appear as pull quotes, margin notes or short interruptions between sections.

Themes already grounded in Ronak's experience:

- AI should solve a meaningful problem, not be included for its own sake.
- A working product matters more than a fancy interface.
- Human review remains necessary in agentic systems.
- Better models do not remove the need for systems design.
- Acceptance criteria and end-user involvement should begin earlier in a project.

### 4. Compress the Work section

Seven entries provide a complete chronology but risk making the homepage feel like an expanded resume. A future iteration could give more space to EY, ProjectHero, Jar and DeepConverse, then group Botspace, Egregore Labs and SeenIt.in under Earlier career.

The full chronology would remain available in the downloadable resume.

## Suggested constraint for a future pass

Aim to make the current homepage roughly 20–25% bolder without changing its information architecture:

- Keep About, At a glance, Work and Projects.
- Add no more than one strong line per section.
- Prefer visual contrast over additional prose.
- Use only opinions and language that Ronak would comfortably say aloud.
- Preserve the existing detailed project case studies.
